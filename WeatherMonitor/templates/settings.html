About HomeWeather

HomeWeather is a real-time weather monitoring web application designed to track and display environmental data collected from home sensors.

Features

  • Connect to ESP8266 sensors for temperature, humidity, rain, and air quality monitoring
  • Real-time dashboard with current readings and historical trends
  • Customizable alerts via email and SMS
  • Anomaly detection
  • Advanced ML-based predictions for future conditions

Supported Sensors

  • DHT22 (Temperature & Humidity)
  • YL-38 (Rain sensor)
  • MQ-135 (Air quality sensor)
